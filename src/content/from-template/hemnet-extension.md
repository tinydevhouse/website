## Overview

Hemnet Extension adds practical decision support to property listings on hemnet.se. The goal is to make housing comparisons easier without moving the workflow away from the listing page.

## What it does

The extension estimates the monthly cost difference compared with current housing, calculates commute distances by transport mode, and adds a summary block to each listing.

## Current focus

The current work is on making the summary useful at a glance, keeping calculations transparent, and avoiding a cluttered listing page.
